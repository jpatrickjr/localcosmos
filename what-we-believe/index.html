What we believe

We believe local is where life happens.

  • The places we visit.
  • The people we meet.
  • The businesses we support.
  • The moments we remember.

Vibrant communities grow when people have more opportunities to discover, connect, and participate.

Our mission

One moment of happiness, every day.

What guides us

Our five beliefs.

  1. 01

    Local life deserves to be discovered.

    Great experiences shouldn't remain hidden simply because people don't know where to look.

  2. 02

    Trust makes communities stronger.

    Reliable information, honest relationships, and human judgment should be foundational.

  3. 03

    Every local business deserves an opportunity to thrive.

    Visibility and connection shouldn't be reserved for businesses with the biggest marketing budgets.

  4. 04

    Communities are built with people, not just technology.

    Technology should amplify local knowledge, relationships, and participation—not replace them.

  5. 05

    When local communities flourish, everyone benefits.

    We measure progress by the opportunities and connections we help create, not simply by the amount of data we collect.

One vision, four connected parts

How we put our beliefs to work.

  • LOIS

    Understand Local

    We build trustworthy local intelligence that helps communities understand their businesses, events, places, and opportunities.

  • The Pull Up

    Discover Local

    We help people find experiences, businesses, and activities worth exploring in their communities.

  • FestiveFly

    Participate Local

    We're building tools that make it easier for people to discover, organize, and participate in local experiences.

  • Trukfoo

    Connect Local

    We connect food trucks with customers, neighborhoods, events, and places where mobile food businesses can thrive.

    A real-world, industry-specific expression of our belief that every local business deserves an opportunity to thrive.
