The opportunity

What Opportunities Are Hiding in Your Destination?

Every destination has valuable local assets that aren't always visible or connected. That's not a shortcoming. It's where the next opportunity often starts.

  • Businesses missing from local inventories
  • Experiences visitors have not discovered
  • Events needing greater visibility
  • Local partnerships waiting to happen
  • Inconsistent business and attraction information
  • Underrepresented community assets

The information challenge

Your Destination Changes Faster Than Your Information.

Businesses open, relocate, change services, and sometimes close. Events and visitor experiences evolve.

Meanwhile, destination information is distributed across many places, and keeping those sources aligned requires continual effort.

In stakeholder discovery in Culpeper, Virginia, one challenge came up again and again: local information keeps changing, but there's rarely a dependable process for noticing, confirming, and sharing those changes.

Local Cosmos combines on-the-ground research, facilitated collaboration, and a developing intelligence foundation to help destination partners see what exists, connect the right people, and act on what they learn.

Service 02

Bring the Right People Together. Create New Possibilities.

Proposed investment

What it is

Inspired by community-centered destination development and participatory design practices, Local Circles bring tourism leaders, small business owners, residents, and community stakeholders together to identify shared challenges and co-design practical opportunities.

Designed for everyone at the table

Sessions are planned for meaningful participation, with attention to accessibility and inclusive engagement, so every voice in the room can contribute.

Client outcome

A clearer shared understanding of a challenge and practical opportunities to address it.

Proposed deliverables

  • Stakeholder and session planning
  • One facilitated, approximately 90–120-minute collaborative session
  • Shared challenge and opportunity identification
  • Guided ideation and prioritization

Technology foundation

LOIS: The Intelligence Foundation Behind the Work

LOIS (Local Operating Intelligence System) is our developing intelligence foundation.

It's being designed to organize local information, preserve source evidence, support verification, and help reveal opportunities, with people making the final call.

What LOIS is being designed to do

Organize

  • Connected local records. Links businesses, places, events, and organizations.
  • Source evidence. Keeps track of where each piece of information came from.

Verify

  • Evidence gathering. Collects the supporting details behind each record or potential change.
  • Human verification. People confirm what's true before a record is updated.

Reveal

  • Opportunity signals. Helps surface gaps, overlooked assets, and possible connections.
  • Feedback and learning. Improves from corrections and outcomes over time.

Included today

A manually curated Destination Intelligence Snapshot, delivered as part of the Destination Ecosystem Assessment.

Planned capability

An interactive client dashboard. Access and delivery are confirmed only after technical readiness and client scope are established.

Not offered today

Automatic monitoring, live data synchronization, or a production-ready dashboard. LOIS is in active development, and people stay in the loop.
