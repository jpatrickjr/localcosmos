The opportunity

What Opportunities Are Hiding in Your Destination?

Every destination has valuable local assets that aren't always visible or connected. That's not a shortcoming. It's where the next opportunity often starts.

  • Businesses missing from local inventories
  • Experiences visitors have not discovered

  • Local partnerships waiting to happen

The information challenge

Your Destination Changes Faster Than Your Information.

Businesses open, relocate, change services, and sometimes close. Events and visitor experiences evolve.

Meanwhile, destination information is distributed across many places, and keeping those sources aligned requires continual effort.

In stakeholder discovery in Culpeper, Virginia, one challenge came up again and again: local information keeps changing, but there's rarely a dependable process for noticing, confirming, and sharing those changes.

Local Cosmos is exploring more reliable ways to identify important changes, organize evidence, support verification, and help destination partners maintain current information.

  • Websites
  • Calendars
  • Partner systems
  • Databases
  • Spreadsheets

Works alongside your team

Local Cosmos complements the systems and expertise you already have. It isn't a replacement for your DMS, CRM, or website.

Introducing LOIS

The Intelligence Foundation for More Connected Destinations

LOIS is the developing local intelligence and operations layer within the Local Cosmos ecosystem.

It's being built to help teams notice change, gather evidence, and keep local records current, with people making the final call.

What LOIS is being built to do

Notice

  • Source-aware information. Every record keeps track of where its information came from.
  • Potential change identification. Flags signals that something may have changed.
  • Evidence gathering. Collects the supporting details behind each potential change.

Confirm

  • Human verification. People confirm what's true before a record is updated.
  • Connected local records. Links businesses, places, events, and organizations.

Share & learn

  • Approved distribution. Shares only approved information, to the channels you choose.
  • Feedback and learning. Improves from corrections and outcomes over time.

The first step

Start With a Local Intelligence Sprint.

A focused first step toward understanding your destination's information gaps and opportunities.

Proposed investment

$2,500

A defined discovery and intelligence engagement built around one practical local-information problem.

Who it's for

Tourism offices, destination marketing and management organizations, and convention and visitors bureaus with a specific local-information question they want answered.

Proposed activities

  1. Define a focused destination information challenge.
  2. Review a mutually agreed sample of local businesses, assets, or experiences.
  3. Identify information gaps and potential changes.
  4. Prioritize items requiring verification.
  5. Present findings and practical next-step recommendations.
  6. Discuss opportunities for continued intelligence and destination activation.

Proposed outcomes

  • Clearer understanding of selected information gaps
  • An evidence-informed list of potential changes
  • A prioritized verification or action register
  • Recommendations for improving the selected workflow
  • A discussion of appropriate next steps

What happens next

We review the findings together. If continued intelligence work or destination activation makes sense, we'll talk about what that could look like.

Timeline, scope, and terms are agreed with you before any work begins. Outcomes are not guaranteed.
